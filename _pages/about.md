Hi! I'm Aavishkar Gautam, a Computer Science student at New York University Abu Dhabi with a minor in Applied Mathematics. I'm passionate about AI safety, machine learning security, and building innovative software solutions.

## Research Interests

I'm currently working as a Research Assistant at the Haven Lab at NYUAD, focusing on AI safety research. I am researching frameworks to understand safety mechanisms specifically *Refusal* in Large Language Models (LLM) using [Representation Engineering](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.01405) methodologies. I'm interested in understanding and improving the robustness of machine learning systems.

## What I'm Working On

I'm currently co-authoring research on refusal mechanisms in frontier models and continuing to explore the intersection of AI safety and machine learning security.
